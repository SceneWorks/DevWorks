## Projects

### DevWorks

DevWorks is a multi-crate Rust workspace that implements a developer tooling platform with authority-controlled filesystem operations, SQLite-backed persistent storage, and an extensible package/addon contribution system. The `devworks-adapters` crate provides concrete implementations of core ports (filesystem destructive execution, verified reads, SQLite storage for artifacts/authority/configuration/repositories), while `devworks-contract-probe` and `eval-*` crates provide contract evaluation tooling and vector-store benchmarking infrastructure respectively. The project enforces safety invariants around destructive filesystem operations (protected roots, worktree cleanup, symlink resolution) and manages authority grants, principal bindings, and configuration proposals through a versioned SQLite store.
