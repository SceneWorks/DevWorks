## Projects

### DevWorks

DevWorks is a multi-crate Rust platform that provides infrastructure for managing AI agent workloads, including filesystem safety enforcement, SQLite-backed persistent storage, authority/grant management, and contract evaluation. The `devworks-adapters` crate implements concrete adapters for filesystem operations (with protected-root safety, verified reads, and destructive execution guards) and SQLite storage (for artifacts, events, grants, repos, authority, and configuration). The `devworks-contract-probe` and `eval-*` crates provide contract evaluation tooling and vector-search workload benchmarking used to assess correctness and performance of the platform's data and query layers.
