Routing behavior

Explicit model / explicit tier / upstream Auto or Auto Beta → direct configured route, no local tier classifier.
New session selecting DevWorks Auto → next eligible SystemOne decision model → Low/Mid/High/Max → allocate and pin a generation model in that tier.
No usable SystemOne decision → next eligible Low model as a pinless auxiliary classifier → same tier validation and generation allocation.
Later turns / tools / compaction / restart → persisted assignment and eligible pin; no classification.
No usable decision or no eligible member of the chosen tier → bounded, explained wait/refusal; no invented tier or hidden cross-tier fallback.

Context, capabilities, effort, enabled state, health and current policy still determine eligibility; do not silently cross to another tier or invent support when a pool is empty.

ROUTE-21 · E42

ROUTE-21 [Confirmed]: Support a configurable pool of multiple SystemOne decision models, separate from conversational tiers. Each new automatically selected logical decision request takes the next eligible pool member in durable round-robin order and never creates or reuses a session pin. Filter by declared question/media/input capabilities, enabled state, usable connection, authorization, availability and current policy. Preserve direct model-ID invocation for callers deliberately selecting a decision model. Concurrent allocation and restart retain the cursor and request identity; receipt replay reuses a known decision rather than dispatching another inference.

ROUTE-22 · E43

ROUTE-22 [Confirmed]: Offer DevWorks Auto as a distinct route selection. Immediately before a new session's first generation request, classify the required tier from its initial prompt, role, task/assignment and authorized project context using a versioned, inspectable rubric. The only valid tier answers are Low, Mid, High and Max. Prefer an eligible SystemOne decision model under ROUTE-21. Explicit named-model and tier selections bypass classification. Explicit upstream auto routes, including OpenRouter Auto and Auto Beta, bypass DevWorks tier classification and retain their configured upstream identity. Preserve existing saved selections and defaults unless explicitly changed; new worker sessions receive their own initial assignment.

ROUTE-23 · E44

ROUTE-23 [Confirmed]: If no SystemOne model is usable for the initial tier decision, use the next eligible Low-tier generation model as an auxiliary classifier through the shared router. This classifier request targets Low directly, has no conversational pin, and cannot recursively invoke tier selection. Treat malformed/non-tier answers, missing capabilities, authentication, exhausted credits, cooldowns, capacity and transport failures honestly. Attempts are bounded per model; other model successes cannot reset a failing model's retry limit. Try eligible decision candidates before falling back, and eligible Low candidates within a bounded pass; if neither path yields a valid decision, wait or refuse with a concrete reason rather than inventing a tier. Attribute classifier and generation attempts separately under existing authority, cost and feature-budget controls.

ROUTE-24 · E45

ROUTE-24 [Confirmed]: Persist a successful DevWorks Auto tier decision and its policy/input provenance, then allocate and pin an eligible generation model within that tier. Classify once per new session, not once per turn. Ordinary messages, tool continuations, compaction, restart, policy edits and later complexity changes must not reclassify or automatically change the tier. Persist an already chosen tier even if generation must wait for availability. Reuse the eligible generation pin; a necessary availability replacement stays within the chosen tier, is explained and persists. If that tier cannot meet a new required capability, surface the limitation without silently changing tier. Manual named-model or tier changes remain explicit exceptions; restarting or resuming work still obeys existing holds. Restoring a previously established automatic assignment never reclassifies it; a manually started session without one needs a new session to obtain an automatic tier assignment. Explicitly auxiliary titles, embeddings and other purpose-routed inference do not establish or invalidate the conversational automatic assignment.

Approved edge policies

  • Keep existing defaults and sessions intact. DevWorks Auto is a new explicit selection, not an automatic migration to Auto.
  • After an explicit override, an existing automatic assignment can be restored without reclassification. A manually started session with no automatic assignment needs a new session for first-prompt Auto; do not retroactively classify its conversation.
  • A successful tier decision survives a generation-availability wait. Availability recovery does not invoke the classifier again.
  • SystemOne and Low fallback use separate decision-purpose allocation from the conversation pin; healthy discovery does not prove working credentials or credits.
  • On an empty chosen generation tier or a new unsupported capability, wait/refuse with a concrete reason; no hidden cross-tier fallback.
  • Explicitly auxiliary titles, embeddings and other purpose-routed inference do not establish or invalidate the conversational Auto assignment.
