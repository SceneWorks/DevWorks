# Access and safety settings

Inspect the effective access profile and source before enabling native operations. Full access retains harness restrictions; project-scoped access is brokered and has a different guarantee. Read-only reviewer enforcement, protected branches, exact human approvals, project lifecycle and current root controls cannot be disabled by role instructions. A proposed change does not take effect until its required decision and adoption boundary.

## Apply a change

Choose the intended scope, read the effective value/source, edit, validate, and save or propose through the app. Keep the original draft and operation identity if the result is uncertain. For a revision conflict, refresh and explicitly review/rebase before retrying. Adoption timing and harness-enforced restrictions are listed in the [Complete settings reference](help:settings-reference).

See [Settings overview](help:settings), [Permissions](help:permissions), and [Recovery](help:recovery).
